Home look-dev
-------------
`home-plate.png` and this `three.min.js` may remain in the tree as
unused look-dev / leftover vendor. Runtime Home is an original HTML
studio tab bar on Obsidian `#05070A` (vertical blades, cyan-teal tips,
soft drop shadows). No galaxy, nebula, or sun is drawn. No NASA logo
is drawn. No WebGL is required for Home.
