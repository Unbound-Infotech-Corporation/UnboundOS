Home look-dev plate
-------------------
`home-plate.png` is an owner-supplied look-dev reference (edge-on linear
galaxy). JWST-class photographs may inform the deep-field palette
offline. Neither is loaded as the runtime Home background. Runtime Home
is an original procedural living galaxy (tilted canvas-painted disk +
moving starfield + original filaments). No NASA or observatory credit
is claimed.
