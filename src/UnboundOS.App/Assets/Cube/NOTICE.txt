Home look-dev plate
-------------------
`home-plate.png` is an owner-supplied look-dev reference (edge-on linear
galaxy). Hugging Face Flux schnell stills, JWST-class photographs, and
a live Wallpaper Engine desktop may inform volume, nebula color, and
the winding energy trail offline.
None of those plates or workshop files are loaded or shipped.
Runtime Home is an original procedural living galaxy (tilted
canvas-painted disk + in-band nebula + moving starfield). The zoomed
category sun is an original shader (granulation, limb darkening,
restrained gold corona). NASA SDO AIA/HMI stills were look-dev only
and are not loaded or shipped. No NASA logo is drawn.
