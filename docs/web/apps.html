Flagship

UnboundOS — the gaming session shell for Windows 11

UnboundOS doesn't replace Windows. It snapshots your machine, strips the noise for the session, and hands your desktop back exactly as it was.

01 / SESSION ENGINE

Enter a posture, not a mess

One click enters a profile: NIC metrics are snapshotted, denylisted background apps are terminated, and games plus anticheat are hard-protected. Exit restores everything.

02 / NETWORK DIRECTOR

Dual-NIC streamer split

Prefer a dedicated game NIC with a low metric while stream and bulk traffic ride a second NIC. Your hits register; your bitrate doesn't fight them.

03 / STREAM CANVAS

Ultrawide → Twitch, no compromise

Exact crop math from ultrawide (default 5110×1400) to a centered 16:9 → 1080p60 Twitch canvas, with an OBS filter guide. It never asks you to lower your monitor resolution.

04 / MODS + WORKSHOP

Local Workshop, handled safely

Discovers installed Steam Workshop content without touching your credentials. Per-game mod profiles with transactional, backed-up apply — and automatic restore if anything fails.

05 / PROFILES

Competitive · Streamer · Living Room

Ships with three tuned JSON profiles — aggressive competitive cleanup, streamer split with the full crop plan, and a calm big-picture living-room shell.

06 / TELEMETRY

Live signal in the header

CPU, memory, process, and suspect counts stream live in the shell header, so you always know what the session is holding back.
